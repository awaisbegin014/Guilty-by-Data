# 🔍 Guilty By Data: The Ashworth Affair
## Competition Introductions & Lore

---

### 🏛️ Main Dashboard (Story Intro)

**Title**
The Ashworth Affair

**Description**
Victor Ashworth, billionaire founder of Ashworth Pharmaceuticals, has been murdered.

**Objective**
Identify the killer, the weapon, and the motive from the digital wreckage.

**Case Brief**
Victor Ashworth was found poisoned with a rare chemical called thallium sulfate. The doctor says the poison was given to him 3 to 4 days before he died. This means the killer planned it carefully. The killer works inside the company and knows a lot about chemicals. We have grabbed three big sets of data from the company's computers: employee records, money transactions, and secret emails. We need you to look at the clues. Follow the data. Find out who wanted him dead, how they got the poison, and who pulled the trigger.

---

### 💻 Online Coding Environment (OCE) Round Introductions

#### 🔴 Round 1

**Title**
Phase I: The Suspect Matrix

**Description**
Profile the personnel and identify persons of interest.

**Objective**
Clean the HR database, engineer a Risk Score, and isolate the top 20 suspects.

**Case Brief**
We have a massive list of over 1,200 employees, but the data is messy and full of copies. The killer is hiding somewhere on this list. Your job is to clean up this data first. Then, find the people who are already marked as "Under Investigation." Finally, you will score them based on their salary, overtime, and performance to see who is the biggest risk. We need you to cut this list down to the top 20 suspects.

---

#### 🔴 Round 2

**Title**
Phase II: The Lethal Ledger

**Description**
Conduct forensic analysis on employee financial records.

**Objective**
Isolate the poison window and locate the lethal transaction.

**Case Brief**
We now have our top 20 suspects. Next, we need to follow the money. The medical timeline shows the poison was given to Victor between October 10th and 11th. You must look at what these suspects bought during those exact dates. Don't let big, normal business expenses trick you. You are hunting for something very specific: hidden chemical purchases, lab tools, or strange vendors. Check their company credit cards and find out who bought the deadly poison.

---

#### 🔴 Round 3

**Title**
Phase III: Encrypted Shadows

**Description**
Analyze internal communication patterns for criminal intent.

**Objective**
Detect encrypted stealth bursts and calculate the final Guilt Multiplier.

**Case Brief**
Only 10 suspects are left. To catch the killer, we need to look at their digital footprints. Criminals do not talk openly. They use secret, locked messages sent very quickly in the dark. We call these "Stealth Bursts." The computer logs we have are broken and hard to read. You must fix the logs, find out who sent the most secret messages, and check for angry words. Put all the clues together, do the final math, and catch Victor's killer once and for all.
